# knowledge/istqb/ — ISTQB notes (distilled)

Your distilled notes from ISTQB study and practical application.
**Not** the official syllabus copy-pasted — your own
re-explanations.

## What goes here

- Definitions in your own words
- Mental models for test design (e.g. equivalence partitioning,
  boundary value analysis, decision tables, state transition,
  exploratory testing)
- Heuristics you've found useful (HICCUPPS-F, FAILURE, RCRCRC,
  SFDPOT, etc.) — phrased in a way you'd explain to a teammate
- Connections between ISTQB concepts and real situations from
  your work (but no client identifiers — see portability rule)

## What does NOT go here

- The official syllabus PDF or copy
- Sample exam questions from copyrighted material
- Anything that violates ISTQB's terms
