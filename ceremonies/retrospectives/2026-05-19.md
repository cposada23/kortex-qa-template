## Patterns I want to remember

> _(promote any of these to `knowledge/patterns/<slug>.md` once
> they recur across clients — see
> [../../playbooks/client-rotation.md](../../playbooks/client-rotation.md)
> for the portability sanitization rule)._

- **Premature subtask check-off** — when subtasks have ambiguous
  "done" criteria, they get marked done before the work is real.
  Mitigation: bind subtask completion to an artifact (test cases
  reviewed = a `reviews/<TICKET>.md` file exists; not just a
  Jira checkbox).
- **Silent env outages** — QA pipeline depends on env stability,
  but env owners don't always notify QA when they take it down.
  Mitigation: explicit Teams channel posts, or auto-monitor that
  pings QA on health-check failure.

## Decisions for next sprint

- Adopt the AC audit subtask reinforcement.
- Trial the env outage notification protocol with DevOps.
